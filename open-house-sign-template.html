Free printable open house sign

Choose an arrow direction, add your hours or address, and print a sign for the day. The SVG download is editable in a vector editor.

Printing tips

Use US Letter paper in landscape orientation. Turn off browser headers and footers for a clean page. Print one copy first and check that the arrow points the right way from where visitors will see it.
