01 / GET YOUR BEARINGS

Three places to start.

Build a shortlist, visit in person, and compare the things that matter to you. These are starting points for research, not neighborhood rankings.

01 ALBUQUERQUE, NM

Nob Hill

Imagine your everyday routine.

Pick your regular destinations, then test the trip from an address you are considering. Try it at the times you would actually travel.

02 ALBUQUERQUE, NM

Old Town

Look beyond your first impression.

Visit on different days and at different times. Note the street activity, parking, noise, and routes you would use in daily life.

Read about Old Town ↗
03 ALBUQUERQUE, NM

Downtown

Try a day in the neighborhood.

Walk a potential errand route. Check the specific services you rely on, their opening hours, and how you would get home afterward.
